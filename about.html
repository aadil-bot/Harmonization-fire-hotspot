THE METHOD · CANADA · 2002–2025

Two sensors.
One comparable record.

MOSAIC combines fire observations from MODIS and VIIRS to make long-term activity easier to compare. It corrects for a sensor change that can look like a sudden increase in fire even when the difference comes from how the satellites observe it.

01 · THE SENSOR CHANGE

Why harmonize the observations?

MODIS and VIIRS do not see fire at the same detail. When VIIRS enters the record, raw hotspot counts rise partly because the instrument can see smaller fires—not only because fire activity changed.

02 · HOW THE DATA IS HARMONIZED

From detections to a shared scale

Each step puts the sensors on a comparable footing while keeping the source and limits of the observations visible.

  1. 01

    Clean and filter

    Keep presumed vegetation fires and nominal- or high-confidence observations. MODIS confidence percentages are mapped to VIIRS-style classes first: below 30% is low, 30–79% nominal, and 80% or higher high. Low-confidence detections are excluded.

  2. 02

    Use the same grid and time unit

    Snap locations to a shared 0.1° grid—about 11 km north–south and 5–7 km east–west. Count a fire cell-day when a cell has at least one fire observation on a date. This reduces the effect of different native pixel sizes.

  3. 03

    Estimate a factor for each month

    Across years when both sensors operated, compare their daily fire-cell totals separately for each calendar month. The VIIRS-to-MODIS ratio becomes that month’s calibration factor.

  4. 04

    Build one historical series

    Before 2012, multiply each MODIS fire-cell-day by its month’s factor. From 2012 onward, use VIIRS on its own as the target scale, avoiding duplicate counts from the overlap.

  5. 05

    Summarize and compare

    Sum harmonized activity into daily series, calendars, and historical trends. For the weekly anomaly series, compare each week with that same week in other years after a log transform; median and median absolute deviation reduce the influence of extreme years.

Select a monthThe monthly factor shows how MODIS fire-cell activity is weighted onto the VIIRS comparison scale.

Worked example: the July factor is about 2.42. Ten MODIS fire-cell-days in July become about 24.2 VIIRS-scale activity units (10 × 2.42). This adjusts the total; it does not recreate small fires MODIS never detected.

How the method is checked: the processing notebook also describes a hold-out check: fit factors on 2012–2018, apply them to MODIS in 2019 onward, then compare with VIIRS observations. The factor chart shows the calibration inputs, not a claim that every individual fire is detected by both sensors.

How the monthly factors are stabilized: each month’s raw VIIRS-to-MODIS ratio is blended with the overall ratio. The monthly weight is MODIS cell-days ÷ (MODIS cell-days + 200), so months with fewer observations are pulled more toward the overall factor. The notebook’s hold-out test reports annual percentage error and weekly correlation; the published factors alone do not include those validation scores.

04 · INTERPRETING THE RESULT

What harmonization can—and cannot—tell you

Comparable totals

Use harmonized cell activity to compare dates and years across the sensor transition. The app’s MODIS and VIIRS modes are available when you need to inspect sensor-specific observations.

Older maps are still sparser

A multiplier can adjust totals, but it cannot add locations for small fires MODIS did not see. Pre-2012 map patterns are calibrated estimates and have less spatial detail.

Not burned area

A fire cell-day means at least one detection somewhere in a roughly 0.1° grid cell on that day. It is not the area burned and does not confirm a fire perimeter.

Other caveats

FRP is not calibrated between sensors; raw detection counts are not comparable; 2002 is partial because the archive begins July 4; a few persistent winter hotspots may be industrial heat sources. The source includes MODIS Aqua only, not Terra. VIIRS low-confidence detections were absent from the supplied source data.

DATA AND REPRODUCIBILITY

Where the record comes from

Satellite active-fire observations are from NASA FIRMS: MODIS Collection 6.1 on Aqua and VIIRS 375 m on Suomi-NPP. Processing includes confidence filtering, common-grid aggregation, monthly overlap calibration, and historical calendar/anomaly summaries.
